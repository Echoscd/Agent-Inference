## Findings
- **Experiments 17 and 18 are invalid.** The router was constructed before the
  config was applied, so `--policy` was silently ignored and *both arms ran the
  default `size` policy*. They sat in `result/` looking like data. This is the
  origin of the worklog rule about recording invalid runs.
- `fidelity` is not a win: on both models it is worse on tail latency than the
  plain `size` baseline, decisively so on Qwen3-32B.
- Temperature 0 does **not** make the two arms reproduce turn for turn — vLLM
  batch variance makes them drift. The A/B is two samples of a workload, not a
  paired comparison.

## Decisions
- Scheduling logic moved out of the router into `SchedulingPolicy` classes under
  `ThunderAgent/ThunderAgent/scheduling/`; a policy is one file plus one factory
  line, and the router keeps no policy branches.
- The A/B harness restarts vLLM before each arm, so both start cold.
- Arm B replays arm A's decode lengths via `X-Decode-Len`, so a value-based
  policy can score by the real upcoming decode instead of a guess.
