## Findings
- The existing `results_*_summary.json` numbers are whole-run: they include the
  cold ramp *and* the drain tail. Dropping warmup alone moves throughput ~2–3%;
  dropping the **drain tail** moves it +33–49% on the coder runs and only +4–6%
  on the Qwen3-32B runs, because the coder runs spend roughly half their wall
  clock draining.
- Across three coder replicates, throughput and p95 deltas are **not
  significant** and change sign between runs (p95: +31.7%, −20.8%, −20.8%; sd
  24.7 points against a mean of −3.3%).
- Resolve is consistent in a way the timing metrics are not: hazard_grade wins
  in all three replicates (14/11, 16/12, 14/11), and wall clock is never worse.
  The plausible story is goodput, not token throughput — but with n=3 and
  trajectory drift in the mix, it is not established.
- `vllm_dev/` (1.1 GB) is **stock, unmodified vLLM 0.12.0**: the four files in
  its `_pristine_backup/` are byte-identical to the live tree, so the planned
  scheduler/KV edits were never made and every recorded run used stock vLLM.

## Decisions
- **One metric layer.** `algorithm/run_metrics.py` owns every definition:
  percentiles, the tape parser, the timeline, the warmup rule, throughput
  attribution. Four call sites now delegate to it; three different percentile
  formulas existed before, one of which did not interpolate. Verified by
  re-checking 1584 metric values byte-identical across 22 run-arms.
- **Warmup rule:** warmup ends when KV utilisation first reaches 90% of that
  run's own peak. Relative to the run, so a policy with a lower steady KV level
  is not penalised. Sensitivity across 0.7–0.95 is ~1% on throughput, ~2% on p95.
- **Tapes now record absolute timestamps** (`t_start_s`). Older runs reconstruct
  the timeline by cumsum, which omits client-side build/eval time between turns;
  each row's `timeline` field says which applied.
- **Repo prepared for release**: monorepo (real system + simulator), summary
  statistics and figures committed, raw tapes and testbeds excluded. All
  machine-specific paths removed.
