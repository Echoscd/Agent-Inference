## Findings
- Effective concurrency is bounded by the **KV pool**, not compute: 258k tokens /
  16152 blocks on this card.
- At 80-way the system is not merely slow, it is thrashing: LRU eviction plus
  recompute-preemption destroys the prefix cache that made 32-way efficient.
- CPU offload is a **negative result** at this operating point. It converts a
  crash into working-but-still-thrashing, and costs more preemptions. It would
  only pay at moderate oversubscription (~48-way) where GPU alone overflows but
  GPU+CPU fits.

## Decisions
- vLLM pinned to **0.12.0**: 0.23 needs a CUDA-13 driver and the host is 12.8.
- 80-way with prefix caching on is the standard operating point — it is the
  regime where the scheduling question actually exists.
- The problem to attack is *which program holds KV*, not *how much KV there is*.
